Privacy — Akins Notes and Backup

Akins Notes and Backup is a private tool used only by John Akins, owner of Akins Fresh Market. It connects to John's own Google Drive to back up his business files and to read and organize his own notebook pages.

  • It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.
  • It reads and writes only John's own files, in the folders he chose.
  • Nothing it reads is sold, shared, or used for advertising.
  • John can remove its access at any time from his Google account's security settings.
