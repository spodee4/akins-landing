Delete your account

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Tap Ask to delete my account.

The request goes straight to us. We finish it within 30 days and email you when it is done. Your points, punch cards and clipped coupons are lost, so use them first. Can't get into the app? Email us from the address on your account and we will do it for you. What we keep after deletion, and why, is in the app privacy policy, section 5.

Common questions

A price in the app is different from the shelf.

Prices can differ by store, so check that the app is set to your store. The register price is what you pay; if something looks wrong, ask the cashier or a manager.

How do I turn off Ginger's AI answers?

Tap Account and switch off AI answers. Nothing more is sent to the AI service. How Ginger works is in the app privacy policy, section 4.
