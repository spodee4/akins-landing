App privacy policy

How the Akins Fresh Market app handles your information.

Last updated November 14, 2026.

The short version. The app uses your information to run your account, your store card, your coupons and points, and to answer questions you choose to ask. We do not sell it. We do not use it for ads. The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and it does not use your location.

This policy covers the Akins Fresh Market app for iPhone ("the app"). It is published by Akins Foods, Inc. ("we", "us"), 106 F St SW, Quincy, WA 98848. Our Oroville and Ritzville stores are run by our related company, Akins Foods – Oroville, Inc.; it handles your information under this same policy. This policy covers the app only. Our other websites and the in-store register have their own practices.

1. Who holds your information

  • Us. Akins Foods, Inc. decides how your information from the app is used.
  • Our online store system. Your account, store card number, clipped coupons, points, punch cards, shopping list and purchase records are kept in our online store system, ECRS WebCart. ECRS runs it for us as our service provider. The app talks to it directly.
  • Our own cloud account. The shopping helper (Ask and Ginger) runs through a connector in our own Google Cloud account. More in section 4.
  • The company that built the app builds it for us under contract. It does not receive or keep your account, your questions, your shopping data or any other personal information from the app.

Every company that handles your information for us does so only to provide its service to us, and is expected to protect it at least as well as this policy does.

3. What we never do

  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising.
  • We do not use what you buy, search for or ask to work out anything about your health.
  • We do not let the AI service use your questions to train its models.

4. Ask, Ginger and the AI service

Ask and Ginger are the app's shopping helper. Nothing is sent to the AI service until you see the permission screen and tap I agree. You can turn AI answers off any time in Account.

When you ask, the app sends your question (and, if you used them, your photo or pasted recipe text) with this week's store prices for the items involved. Your name, card number, sign-in, email, phone and location are not sent.

The answer is written by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, PBC, run for us on Google Cloud (Vertex AI) in our own Google Cloud account. If that path is down, the same model is reached through our own account with Anthropic directly. Our connector does not store your questions, photos or the answers.

Google and Anthropic do not use your questions to train their AI models. Either of them may hold a request for a short time, up to 30 days, to check for misuse and keep the service safe, and then delete it.

Please do not type health details, card numbers or other private facts into Ginger. Ginger helps with shopping; it does not give medical, allergy or nutrition advice.

5. How long we keep information

  • Your account and its records: while your account is open.
  • After you delete your account: we remove your account, card link, coupons, points, punch cards, list and search history. We keep only records of completed sales that tax and accounting law require us to keep, and only for as long as that law requires. They are no longer tied to an app account.
  • Ask and Ginger questions: not stored by us. See section 4 for the AI service.
  • Messages to the store: kept as long as we need them to help you, and removed with your account.

6. Your choices and rights

  • AI answers: off any time in Account. Nothing more is sent once it is off.
  • Delete your account: in the app, tap Account, then Delete account, then Ask to delete my account. The request goes straight to us. We finish it within 30 days and email you when it is done. Your points, punch cards and clipped coupons are lost. You can also ask by email (below). Step-by-step help is on the app help page.
  • See, fix or get a copy of your information: email us. We answer within 30 days. We will check that the request comes from the account holder before we act on it.
  • Notifications: on or off in your iPhone's Settings.

We give these choices to every customer, wherever you live.

7. Children

The app is for people 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, email us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Your sign-in is kept in your iPhone's keychain. The app talks to our systems over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure; if something goes wrong that affects your information, we will tell you as the law requires.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will post the new version here and change the date at the top. If a change means your information is used in a new way, we will ask you in the app first.
